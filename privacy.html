Privacy policy

Pie Fixe is an appliance repair business based in Rosettenville, Johannesburg, South Africa. We aim to collect only what we need to respond to an enquiry and arrange or discuss a repair.

By using this website, you acknowledge this policy. Where the law requires consent, we will ask for it through the relevant form, tool, or service.

1. What we collect

When you use our contact or booking forms, we may collect your name, email address, optional phone number, appliance type, repair message, and the page or source that led to the enquiry.

If you contact us through WhatsApp, WhatsApp receives the information you choose to send there and handles it under its own privacy policy.

We may also receive limited technical information needed to operate and protect the website, such as browser, device, approximate location, and server or security logs.

2. Why we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to you, understand the appliance problem, provide a quote or next step, arrange a visit, keep business records, prevent spam, and improve our service. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who may receive it

Enquiries are sent to Pie Fixe and stored in our website database service, Supabase. Where configured, enquiry details are sent through our email provider to the Pie Fixe business inbox, and an automatic confirmation may be sent to the email address you provide.

Our website also links to WhatsApp and embeds an OpenStreetMap map on the Contact page. Those services may process information when you choose to use them or load the relevant page. Their own privacy policies apply to that processing.

We may disclose information where required by law, to protect the website or business, or to service providers who process it on our instructions.

4. Cookies and similar tools

The website uses essential browser functionality and may use cookies or similar technologies supplied by Google Tag Manager or tags configured within it. The current site does not use a customer account or advertising profile.

Google Fonts may also request resources from Google when fonts load. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies or external resources may affect parts of the website.

5. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry records only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, provide services, manage business records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and meet legal or accounting obligations. Retention may vary depending on the enquiry and any resulting repair relationship.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask what personal information we hold about you, request correction, ask us to delete or restrict use where appropriate, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also raise a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect enquiry information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information through a web form or WhatsApp.

8. Policy updates

We may update this policy when the website, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
