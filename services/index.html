Before you book

Useful answers,
up front.

Can I send a photo first?

Yes. A photo of the appliance label, display, error code, or visible issue can help us prepare before a visit.

Will I know the cost before work starts?

We explain the diagnosis and quote before proceeding with repair work. If the appliance is not worth repairing, we will say so plainly.

Do you work outside Rosettenville?

Yes. Pie Fixe serves Johannesburg and surrounding cities. Send your suburb with your enquiry and we will confirm the visit area.
