How to play

  1. Players take turns placing one orb in an empty cell or on their own orbs.
  2. Every cell has a critical mass — the number of cells touching it: corners 2, edges 3, middle 4.
  3. When a cell reaches critical mass it explodes, flinging an orb into each neighbor and capturing them.
  4. Explosions chain — one good move can flip half the board.
  5. Lose all your orbs and you're eliminated. Last color standing wins!
